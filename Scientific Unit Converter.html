Two-state populations

ΔG° = RT ln(Kd/c°) with standard state c° = 1 mol/L. An IC50 equals Ki only under specific assay conditions (Cheng–Prusoff), so convert it with care.

Conventions: cal and kcal are thermochemical (1 cal = 4.184 J exactly), as used by AMBER, CHARMM and NAMD. GROMACS works in nm, ps, kJ/mol, bar and e.
